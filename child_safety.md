# Child Safety Standards Policy
Scaape is committed to providing a safe and responsible platform for users aged 16 and older. We recognize the need to protect young users from harmful content, ensure appropriate interactions, and comply with relevant child safety laws.

This Child Safety Standards Policy outlines Scaape’s approach to safeguarding users under 18 while maintaining a seamless experience for all.

## Age Restrictions & Eligibility

Scaape is intended for users who are 16 years or older. Users under the age of 16 are not permitted to create an account, attend, or host events. Certain features, including access to events containing adult content, alcohol consumption, gambling, or other age-restricted activities, will be exclusively available to users 18 years and older. While parental consent is not legally required in most jurisdictions for users aged 16 and above, we encourage parental awareness and guidance.

If we discover an account registered by someone under 16, we will immediately terminate the account and remove any associated data.

## Content Moderation and Messaging

We prohibit any content, interaction, or activity that may endanger minors or expose them to inappropriate material. Users under 18 are restricted from viewing, registering for, or participating in any event marked “18+” or containing explicit content. User profiles for individuals under 18 are set to private by default, ensuring enhanced privacy and limiting access to personal information.

Scaape enforces strict event moderation policies to ensure safe participation. Any event intended for teen users must be labeled as “Teen-Safe”, and organizers must comply with Scaape’s community guidelines. Events featuring adult entertainment, gambling, explicit content, or excessive alcohol promotion will be inaccessible to users under 18. Organizers and attendees found violating these policies may face content removal, account suspension, or permanent bans.


To further protect younger users, we implement robust reporting and enforcement mechanisms. Users can report any inappropriate behavior, content, or events through the in-app reporting system or by emailing info@scaape.in, or by contacting our moderation team. Reports related to child safety are prioritized, and actions may include content removal, account suspension, and referral to law enforcement if necessary.

## Data Protection and Enforcement

Scaape is committed to safeguarding user data, particularly for minors. We do not collect unnecessary personal data from users under 18, and tracking is limited to ensure privacy. Parents or guardians may request data access or deletion for users under 18 by contacting privacy@scaape.in. Our platform adheres to global child protection regulations, including GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation - EU) and POCSO (Protection of Children from Sexual Offences - India).

Violations of our child safety standards will result in appropriate enforcement actions. Minor offenses may lead to content removal or temporary account suspension, while serious infractions, such as grooming, harassment, or exploitation, will result in permanent bans and legal action. Scaape actively collaborates with law enforcement agencies to address violations of child safety policies.

For any concerns regarding child safety, users can contact us via the in-app reporting system or email us at info@scaape.in. By using Scaape, all users agree to abide by these child safety policies and acknowledge their responsibility in maintaining a secure and respectful community.
